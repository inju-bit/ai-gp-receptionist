
FICTIONAL GP SURGERY — DEMONSTRATION DATA

Practice name:
Green Valley Medical Centre

Opening hours:
Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM to 6:30 PM.
Saturday and Sunday: Closed.

Appointments:
Patients can request appointments by contacting reception.
Same-day appointments are subject to availability.
Appointments cannot currently be booked directly through this AI chatbot.

Repeat prescriptions:
Patients can request repeat prescriptions through the practice's online services.
Requests normally take two working days to process.
The chatbot cannot prescribe or approve medication.

Test results:
Patients should contact the GP surgery for information about their test results.
The chatbot cannot access patient records or interpret results.

Registration:
New patients should contact reception to ask about registration requirements.

Contact information:
The practice telephone number has not been provided in this demonstration dataset.

Urgent help:
For life-threatening emergencies, call 999.
For urgent medical help that is not life-threatening, NHS 111 may be appropriate.

Data protection:
This is a fictional practice.
No real patient information should be submitted to this demonstration system.
